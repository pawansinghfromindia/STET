## Bihar STET

<details>
  <summary> 1. <b> Bihar STET </b> </summary>

<br/>

**Bihar STET** stands for **Bihar Secondary Teacher Eligibility Test**. <br/> 
It is conducted by the Bihar School Examination Board (BSEB) for candidates seeking eligibility to teach at the secondary and senior secondary levels in Bihar.

---

</details>
<!-------------------------------->

<details>
  <summary> 2. <b> Overview of Bihar STET </b> </summary>

<br/>

| Detail	         | Information                                                   |
|------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------|
| Exam name	       | Bihar Secondary Teacher Eligibility Test (STET)               |
| Conducting body	 | Bihar School Examination Board (BSEB)                         |
| Purpose	         | Teacher eligibility for secondary and senior secondary classes|
| Paper 1	         | Classes 9 - 10                                                |
| Paper 2	         | Classes 11 - 12                                               |
| Exam mode	       | Computer-based test (CBT), as per recent exam patterns        | 
| Question type	   | Objective-type multiple-choice questions (MCQs)               |
| Negative marking | Check the current notification for confirmation               |
| Official website | [BSEB official website](https://bsebstet.org/)                |


Passing STET establishes eligibility; it **does not automatically guarantee a teaching job**. <br/>
Recruitment generally requires a separate selection process i.e. through **TRE**

---

</details>
<!-------------------------------->


<details>
  <summary> 3. <b> Eligibility criteria </b> </summary>

<br/>

**Paper 1; Classes 9 - 10 | Secondary teacher eligibility** <br/> 
Generally, candidates need a graduation degree in the relevant subject and a B.Ed. qualification or another qualification permitted by the notification. Subject-specific requirements apply.


**Paper 2; Classes 11 - 12 | Senior secondary teacher eligibility** <br/> 
Generally, candidates need a postgraduate degree in the relevant subject along with B.Ed. or an applicable equivalent qualification, subject to the official rules.


Important: The exact qualification depends on the subject, degree, marks and applicable relaxation rules. <br/>
Computer Science and certain other subjects may have different requirements.

</details>

<details>
  <summary> 5. <b> Bihar STET Syllabus </b> </summary>

<br/>

The syllabus is divided into two papers, and the subject-specific portion depends on which subject you choose. <br/> 
Each paper has 150 questions for 150 marks, with a duration of 2 hours 30 minutes

**1. Exam pattern**

|         Topics                                           | Marks     |
|----------------------------------------------------------|-----------|
| **Subject-specific section** (Your selected subject)     | 100 marks |
| **Art of Teaching** (Teaching methods and pedagogy)      | 30 marks  |
| **General Knowledge** (General awareness)                | 5 marks   |
| **Environmental Science**                                | 5 marks   |
| **Mathematical Aptitude**                                | 5 marks   |
| **Logical Reasoning**                                    | 5 marks   |
| **Total**                                                | 150 marks |


**2. Common topics**

- **Art of Teaching**: `Teaching-learning process`, `Teaching methods`, `Lesson planning`, `Classroom management`, `Assessment and evaluation`, and `Educational psychology`.

- **General Knowledge**: `Current affairs`, `History`, `Geography`, `Indian polity` and `General awareness`.

- **Environmental Science**: `Environment`, `pollution`, `natural resources and conservation`.

- **Mathematical Aptitude**: `Number system`, `Percentages`, `Ratio` and `Proportion`, `Averages` and `Basic Arithmetic`.

- **Logical Reasoning**: `Series`, `Analogy`, `Classification`, `Coding-decoding` and `Logical problems`.

These are preparation areas; use the official syllabus for the precise topic list.


**3. Subject-wise syllabus**

| Paper 1     | Classes 9 - 10      | `Hindi, English, Mathematics, Science, Social Science` and other listed subjects. |
|-------------|---------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------|


| **Paper 2** | **Classes 11 - 12** | **`Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology, Computer Science, History, Geography, Commerce`, etc** |
|-------------|---------------------|------------------------|

## Strategy for preparation

<details>
  <summary> <b>  Order of preparation </b> </summary>

<br/>

|  Order of Content                  | Subjects                                                            |
|------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **1. Foundations**:                | `Digital Logic`, `Computer Organisation and Architecture`.          |
| **2. Programming**:                | `Programming` and `Data Structures`, `Algorithms`, `OOP`.           |

| **5. Emerging and applied topics**:| `IoT`, `Artificial Intelligence`, `E-Commerce` and `Multimedia`.    |
